\hypertarget{prompts-used}{%
\section{Prompts used}\label{prompts-used}}

\textbf{Prompt 01 (filled).} I am a postgraduate student converting my
completed literature review into an implementation-based research paper.
My topic is \emph{citation fabrication in large language models and
agentic search tools (benchmarking failure modes, evidence and
verification)}. Act as a critical research mentor and technical pair
programmer. Guide me in stages: evidence-based gap identification,
literature-to-experiment mapping, feasibility, research questions,
verified baseline, coding, experimental evaluation, manuscript and
reproducibility. Mark statements as VERIFIED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN, and
explicitly request source files or outputs when evidence is missing.
Never invent papers, datasets, repository links, code execution,
experiments or metrics. Help me create a portable research\_decisions.md
log containing decisions, reasons, source/evidence and next actions.
Start by asking me to attach my existing review and summarize its scope
without assuming unseen content.

\textbf{Prompt 02 (filled).} Analyze my attached literature review
\emph{{[}Benchmarking Citation Fabrication Rates Across Large Language
Models and Agentic Search Tools: A Survey of Failure Modes, Evidence,
and Verification --- attached PDF{]}}. Identify central methods,
datasets, measured findings, author-reported limitations and unresolved
questions. Propose five computationally testable research gaps. For
each, cite the specific passage or reference in my review; clearly
distinguish a limitation reported in the original paper from your own
inference; state the experimental question it suggests and what evidence
would test it. Flag any claims needing verification against the original
publications. Rank three gaps by research significance, available
resources and implementation feasibility. Do not claim novelty without a
separate literature search.

\emph{Tools used:} Claude (Anthropic), including web search to check
selected claims against the original papers. The AI first asked for the
review; the full 23-page PDF was supplied and read before any output was
produced. I read the outputs and accepted the scope summary and gap
ranking. Hardware: laptop (AMD Ryzen 7 260, NVIDIA RTX 5050 laptop GPU,
8 GB VRAM) and Google Colab free tier.
